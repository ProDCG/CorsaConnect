# **1\. Purpose of Agreement**

Client is hiring a Programmer to provide programming, setup, maintenance, support, and feature development services for Client's racing simulator management software used at Ridge Activity Center.

The software is intended to assist with the operation, management, and continued improvement of Client's racing simulator systems and related business operations.

# **2\. Term of Agreement**

This Agreement shall begin on \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_ ("Start Date") and continue for a period of twelve (12) months, unless terminated earlier according to this Agreement.

At the end of the twelve-month term, the Parties may renew this Agreement in writing.

# **3\. Upfront Setup Cost**

Client agrees to pay Programmer an upfront setup cost of $1,500.00.

In addition to the upfront setup cost, Client shall also pay any previous two (2) months' recurring costs that are owed at the time this Agreement begins, if applicable.

Payment of the upfront setup cost is due on or before \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_ ("Setup Payment Due Date"), unless otherwise agreed to in writing by both Parties.

# **4\. Monthly Recurring Cost**

Client agrees to pay Programmer a monthly recurring fee of $43.00 per simulator rig per month.

This monthly recurring fee shall apply for the full twelve-month term of this Agreement.

For example, if Client operates six simulator rigs, the monthly recurring cost would be: 6 rigs x $43.00 \= $258.00 per month.

The monthly recurring fee is due on the \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_ day of each month, unless otherwise agreed to in writing.

# **5\. Additional Game Integration Fee**

If Client requests that a new game be added to the software, the Parties agree that there may be an additional one-time fee.

The amount of this one-time fee shall be negotiated and agreed upon in writing at the time the new game is requested.

Programmer is not required to begin work on adding a new game until the Parties have agreed in writing to the scope, cost, and timeline for that specific game integration.

# **6\. Maintenance and Additional Features**

Maintenance, bug fixes, updates, and additional software features are included in the monthly recurring price, unless otherwise stated in this Agreement.

This includes reasonable programming work related to improving the software, fixing bugs, maintaining current functionality, and adding features requested by Client.

However, if a requested feature, integration, tool, license, API, software service, or other third-party requirement creates an additional cost charged by an outside company, that third-party cost shall be paid by Ridge Activity Center.

Programmer shall notify Client in advance if a requested feature may require a third-party fee.

# **7\. Quarterly Feature and Bug Review Meetings**

The Parties agree to hold quarterly meetings to discuss features Client would like to add, bugs or software issues that need to be fixed, current software performance, priorities for the upcoming quarter, and any major software changes or improvements being considered.

These quarterly meetings may be held in person, by phone, or by video call. The Parties shall work together in good faith to schedule these meetings at a reasonable time.

# **8\. Monthly Checkup Meetings**

In addition to quarterly meetings, the Parties agree to hold monthly checkup meetings.

The purpose of the monthly checkup meetings is to review progress on the current quarter's goals, active bugs or technical issues, recently completed work, upcoming priorities, and any delays, blockers, or needed decisions from Client.

These meetings are intended to help keep software development and maintenance organized throughout each quarter.

# **9\. Scope of Work**

Programmer agrees to provide programming services related to the racing simulator management software, including but not limited to initial setup and configuration, software maintenance, bug fixes, general software improvements, additional features requested by Client, support for currently included software functions, and reasonable updates needed to keep the software operational.

Any major change outside the normal maintenance and improvement of the software may require a separate written agreement between the Parties.

# **10\. Response Time and Communication**

Programmer agrees to respond to reasonable Client communications within \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_ business days.

Client agrees to provide clear information about bugs, requested features, and software issues when possible, including screenshots, descriptions, examples, or instructions to replicate the problem.

# **11\. Ownership, License, and Redistribution Rights**

Programmer shall retain ownership of the software, source code, reusable code, tools, frameworks, templates, libraries, concepts, methods, and general programming knowledge used in or developed under this Agreement, unless the Parties agree otherwise in a separate written agreement.

Programmer retains the right to reuse, modify, license, sell, distribute, and redistribute the program or any portion of the program to other parties, provided that Programmer does not disclose or include Client's confidential information, customer information, private business information, login credentials, internal procedures, Ridge Activity Center branding, or any materials owned by Client without Client's written permission.

Upon payment of all required fees, Programmer grants Client a non-exclusive, ongoing license to use the software and related work product for Client's internal business operations at Ridge Activity Center.

Any Client-provided materials, confidential information, business records, customer information, logos, branding, or other proprietary Client assets shall remain the property of Client.

# **12\. Confidentiality**

Programmer agrees not to disclose, share, sell, or misuse any confidential information belonging to Client.

Confidential information may include, but is not limited to, business operations, customer information, pricing, internal procedures, software systems, login credentials, financial information, and any private information related to Ridge Activity Center.

This confidentiality obligation shall continue after this Agreement ends.

# **13\. Security and Access**

If Programmer is given access to Client's systems, accounts, software, hardware, or business information, Programmer agrees to use that access only for work related to this Agreement.

Programmer shall not share login credentials or access with any unauthorized person without Client's written approval.

Upon termination of this Agreement, Programmer shall return or remove access to Client systems as reasonably requested by Client.

# **14\. Payment Terms**

Invoices shall be sent to Client on a monthly basis unless otherwise agreed.

Client agrees to pay invoices within \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_ days of receiving them.

Late payments may result in delayed work or suspension of services after written notice from Programmer.

# **15\. Independent Contractor Relationship**

Programmer is an independent contractor and is not an employee, partner, or agent of Client.

Programmer is responsible for Programmer's own taxes, insurance, equipment, and business expenses unless otherwise agreed to in writing.

# **16\. Termination**

Either Party may terminate this Agreement with thirty (30) days' written notice.

If Client terminates the Agreement early, Client remains responsible for payment of all work completed, all unpaid monthly recurring fees through the termination date, and any approved third-party fees or negotiated game integration fees.

If Programmer fails to provide services, refuses to fix major software issues, or materially breaches this Agreement, Client may provide written notice describing the issue. Programmer shall have fourteen (14) days to correct the issue. If the issue is not corrected, Client may terminate the Agreement.

# **17\. Effect of Termination**

Upon termination of this Agreement, Programmer shall provide Client with any completed work product paid for by Client, return or delete confidential Client information upon request, Client shall pay any outstanding approved invoices, and Programmer shall assist with a reasonable transition of software access if requested by Client.

# **18\. Limitation of Liability**

Neither Party shall be liable for indirect, incidental, special, or consequential damages, including lost profits, except in cases of fraud, intentional misconduct, confidentiality violations, or misuse of Client information.

# **19\. Changes to Agreement**

Any changes to this Agreement must be made in writing and signed or approved by both Parties.

Verbal changes are not binding unless confirmed in writing.

# **20\. Governing Law**

This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the State of Washington.

# **21\. Entire Agreement**

This Agreement represents the full understanding between the Parties and replaces any prior discussions, promises, or agreements related to the subject of this Agreement.
